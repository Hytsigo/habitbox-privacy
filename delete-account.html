Delete Your HabitBox Account

Last updated: October 10, 2026

To request deletion of your HabitBox account and associated personal data, please contact us using the support email below.

How to request deletion

  1. Email rioscleiver49@gmail.com with the subject “HabitBox account deletion”.
  2. Identify the email address or phone number associated with your HabitBox account. If you can, write from its associated email address. Phone-only accounts can contact us from an email address where they can receive a reply.
  3. Clearly state that you want your HabitBox account and associated data deleted. Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment details or identity documents in the initial request.

What will be deleted

After verifying ownership, we process deletion of your account profile and sign-in information held by HabitBox, habits, completion history and associated app subscription records, subject to any applicable retention exceptions.

We may ask for the minimum additional information needed to verify ownership before deleting data. This email process does not require signing into this website. Sending an email does not immediately or automatically delete the account.

Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations or payment-related issues, as described in the Privacy Policy. Data held by Google or RevenueCat is subject to their policies; this request does not delete your Google account.

Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your HabitBox account, signing out or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage renewal separately in Google Play subscriptions. A deletion request is not a refund request.
