1. Introduction

HabitBox: Habit Tracker is an Android app for tracking habits. The service is presented under the HabitBox brand. To contact the person managing the service and handling questions about your data, write to rioscleiver49@gmail.com.

This policy covers information related to the app. This information website has no forms, authentication, analytics tools or cookies of its own. When served through Vercel hosting, that provider may process technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website.

2. Information collected

Depending on the features you use, HabitBox may process the following information:

Account information

Your name, internal user ID and time zone. Depending on your sign-in method, this also includes your email address, Google identifier and profile photo, or the email address or phone number you provide when registering with a password. For password accounts, we store a protected representation of the password (an Argon2id hash), not the plaintext password.

Habit information

Habits, names, icons or emojis, colors, frequency, selected days, reminder times, active or inactive status, and completion records and dates.

Subscription information

Subscription status, purchased product, relevant dates and identifiers needed for synchronization. We also process information from RevenueCat and Google Play needed to validate Premium access.

Technical data and communications

Requests to our services may generate technical logs, such as IP address, date, requested path and request outcome, to operate, troubleshoot and protect the service. Subscription services may process app, device and transaction information. If you contact us, we process your email address and the content needed to handle your request.

3. How information is used

  • Authenticate you and manage your account.
  • Store and manage habits and completion records.
  • Generate habit-tracking statistics.
  • Configure local reminders according to your preferences and time zone.
  • Validate and synchronize subscriptions and Premium access.
  • Handle questions and account/data deletion requests.

HabitBox uses its own backend to manage accounts, habits, completion records, statistics and subscription status. Backend data is stored in a PostgreSQL database.

4. Authentication and sessions

HabitBox uses Google Sign-In to identify your account. It may receive your name, email address, Google identifier and profile photo, when available. Google handles that authentication; HabitBox does not receive or store your Google password.

You can also create an account using email or phone and a HabitBox password. The password is sent to the backend for verification or hashing. Your phone number is not used to send SMS or OTP messages; contact verification and password recovery are not available in this version.

The app stores the access token in the device's secure storage to restore your session. Signing out removes that local token; it does not delete your account, habits or subscription.

5. Habits and user-created data

Habits and their records are associated with your account to track activity and calculate statistics. You choose what information to include in habit names and settings.

Avoid including sensitive information that is not necessary for the tracking you want to do.

6. Subscriptions and payments

When Premium purchases are available, monthly and annual Android subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. HabitBox uses RevenueCat to manage and validate subscriptions. The backend checks their status to authorize Premium features. Applicable prices and conditions are those shown by the store before purchase confirmation.

These functions use the subscription information described above. HabitBox does not receive or store payment-card numbers or banking credentials. Google Play handles payment-method information under its terms and policies.

Deleting a HabitBox account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play's subscriptions section.

7. Third-party services

The following services may process the information needed for their functions under their own policies:

  • Google Sign-In: authentication and account identification. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: app distribution and Android purchases/subscriptions. See Google's Privacy Policy for its services.
  • RevenueCat: subscription and Premium status management and validation using relevant identifiers and data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: HTTPS access and delivery of requests to the backend. It may process technical data needed to deliver and protect that traffic. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

For technical data processed when this site is served by Vercel, see Vercel's Privacy Policy. These providers may process information in countries other than yours under their policies and applicable rules.

8. Notifications

HabitBox can schedule local notifications on your device through Expo Notifications to remind you of habits. Reminders are generated locally; this feature does not use a remote push-notification system.

You can allow or disable notifications in Android settings. Disabled notifications will not display reminders.

Habit names may appear in notifications and on the lock screen, depending on your Android settings. You can restrict those previews on your device.

9. Data retention

Account information is retained while needed to provide HabitBox's features. You can request deletion of your account and associated data using the contact below.

Some information may need to be retained for applicable legal obligations or to resolve payment-related issues. Information processed by Google and RevenueCat is also subject to their own retention policies.

10. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No data storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your HabitBox account and associated data, including habits and completion records, by emailing the contact address.

State that you want to delete your account and identify its associated email address or phone number. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify ownership. Do not send passwords, access tokens or payment details. See the account deletion instructions.

Possible retention exceptions are described in Data retention. Uninstalling the app is not a deletion request. Cancel any active subscription separately in Google Play.

You may also contact us to request access to or correction of your data, or to exercise rights applicable to your situation.

12. Children's privacy

HabitBox is not specifically directed at children. Users must meet the age requirements applicable to Google Play and the rules applicable to their use of the service.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided data that should be deleted, contact us to request a review.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to HabitBox's features or applicable requirements. The updated version will appear on this page with a revision date. Please review it periodically.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or account and data deletion requests, email rioscleiver49@gmail.com.
